# CieroSportsSales_Dashboard
Interactive sales analytics dashboard for Ciero Sports, visualizing profit, units sold, regional performance, and top product categories using Power BI.

## 📌 Project Overview
The Ciero Sports Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze and monitor the sales performance of a sports goods company. It provides a comprehensive view of profits, units sold, regional trends, and top-performing products — all in one place.


## 🎯 Business Objective
- Track overall sales and profitability across regions and time periods
- Identify top-performing salespersons, products, and categories
- Support data-driven decisions by visualizing monthly and quarterly trends
- Compare regional performance to optimize distribution and marketing strategies



## 📊 Dashboard Features
- **Quarter Filter** – Toggle between Qtr 1, Qtr 2, Qtr 3, and Qtr 4
- **Region Filter** – Filter data by West, East, North, and South
- **Dynamic Charts** – All visuals update based on selected filters
- **Dark Theme UI** – Professional dark-themed dashboard with a clean layout

## 📉 Visualizations
- **Total Profit by Salesperson** – Bar chart comparing Ethan, Charlie, Fiona, Hannah, and George
- **Total Sales by Month** – Line chart showing monthly sales trends (January to November)
- **Total Units Sold by Region** – Horizontal bar chart (West, East, North, South)
- **Total Profit by Region** – Horizontal bar chart comparing regional profitability
- **Top Product Categories** – Bar chart featuring Fitness Equipment, Cricket, Tennis, Football, Basketball
- **Top 5 Products by Product Name** – Bar chart highlighting Treadmill, Pads, Dumbbells, Goal Net, Soccer Ball



## 💡 Business Insights
- **Fitness Equipment** is the highest revenue-generating category, followed by Cricket and Tennis
- **Treadmill** is the best-selling individual product, contributing the most to total sales
- **West region** leads in both units sold and total profit
- Sales show a **peak mid-year** and a decline towards November, suggesting seasonal trends
- Salesperson performance is relatively balanced, with minor variations across the team
